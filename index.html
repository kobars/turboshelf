TurboShelf

The right item is already there.

Press ⌥ Space in any app. TurboShelf shows the replies, links, images and files you use there first. Press Return, and the item goes into the app you were using.

Coming soon on the Mac App Store.

Keep what you reuse

Save text, links, images, files and folders by drag and drop, from the Services menu, or from the + menu. Save Copy keeps an independent copy. Link points to the original file.

Learns where you paste

TurboShelf learns which items you reuse in which app, and says why an item is there, for example “Used 14 times in Keynote”. Pin an item for one app or for every app.

Private by design

Your items stay on your Mac. There is no account and no cloud upload.

Free and Pro

Free has every feature for up to 25 saved items. TurboShelf Pro removes the limit with one purchase. No subscription.
