TurboShelf

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

TurboShelf is a Mac app for saving and reusing text, links, images and files.

Your content stays on your Mac

Everything you save in TurboShelf is stored on your Mac: items, files, text recognized in images and documents, usage history and settings. TurboShelf does not upload it anywhere. TurboShelf has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not track you.

What TurboShelf saves

TurboShelf saves only the items you choose to save. It does not record your clipboard history. To suggest items, it counts on your Mac which items you reuse in which app. That record stays on your Mac.

Purchases

When you buy or restore TurboShelf Pro, Apple processes the payment. TurboShelf uses RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Apple and an anonymous identifier created for the app. It does not receive your content, name or email. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Permissions

TurboShelf asks for the Accessibility permission to paste an item into the app you were using, after you press Return. It does not use this permission to read or record other apps’ content.

Deleting your data

Removing an item in TurboShelf deletes TurboShelf’s copy. It never deletes your original files. To remove everything, quit TurboShelf, delete the app, and delete its folder ~/Library/Containers/com.kobars.turboshelf.

Children

TurboShelf does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
